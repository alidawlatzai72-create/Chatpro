✨ AI Chat Assistant
✨
Your conversations, easier
Get intelligent answers, ask questions about your chats, summarize conversations, translate text, and draft polished replies. Answers use a secure server-side AI connection.

🤖 Ask AI anything

A real AI assistant for explanations, writing, planning, ideas, and everyday questions. It does not send messages unless you choose to use a suggested reply.

💬 Ask your chats

Ask a question about messages your account can access. AI answers using relevant excerpts and tells you when the chat history does not contain enough evidence.

✅ Find tasks & promises

Finds messages that may contain requests, deadlines, follow-ups, or commitments. Check the original message before relying on a task.

🧾 Summarize unread / recent messages

Uses a secure AI model to summarize a selection of messages available to your account. Messages that database permissions do not allow you to read cannot be included.

🔎 Find information in old conversations

Searches message text in accessible direct conversations and locally saved group chats.

🌐 Translate a conversation message

Uses the app's existing free translation service. Free providers can be rate-limited; your original text stays unchanged.

✍️ Generate a professional reply

Creates a suggested reply based on the latest received message in the open direct chat. Review it before sending.

🎙️ Voice message to text

Transcribing an existing audio file requires a speech-to-text engine. This standalone HTML has no speech-to-text service configured, so it cannot reliably convert saved voice notes yet. Browser dictation below can capture new speech where supported.
